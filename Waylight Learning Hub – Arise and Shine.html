Born from a real classroom problem

In 2021, founder Collins Mwangi, a former primary school teacher from Eastlands, Nairobi, noticed how addicted kids were to games and asked: why not build games that actually teach?

Collins teamed up with software engineer Anthony Wai to build the first prototype, piloted at Marurui Primary School and Royal Brains Kinderstart School. Today Shule AI Plus+ serves learners from urban Nairobi to rural Turkana, in English and Kiswahili.
